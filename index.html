Publication

The 3D scanner project was written up and published as a research paper.

3D Scanner

  • International Journal for Research in Applied Science & Engineering Technology (IJRASET), Volume 13, Issue V, May 2025
  • Co-authors: Ashish Singh, Harsh Gautam, Shlesh Choudhary, Ayushi, Sakshi (Shriram Group of Colleges, Muzaffarnagar)
  • An affordable Arduino Uno scanner that uses a Sharp IR sensor, an SD card module and motor control to capture 3D coordinates and turn them into a model. The paper covers calibration, scanning, data storage and processing, and applications in manufacturing, prosthetics and digital twins.
  • Keywords: 3D scanning, Arduino-based scanner, sensor calibration, digital twins, cost-effective modelling
  • Read the paper  |  DOI: 10.22214/ijraset.2025.70429
